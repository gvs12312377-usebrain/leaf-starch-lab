Why Are We Doing This?

The plant was kept in the dark for 48 hours to remove any pre-existing starch. Covering part of the leaf with black foil blocks sunlight from reaching that section, creating a controlled test!

🔥

Lab Safety Checkpoint!

Handling Ethanol Safely

Why use a hot water bath instead of heating ethanol directly?

Ethanol is highly flammable! Heating a test tube of ethanol directly over an open flame or red-hot coil could cause it to catch fire. Using a hot water bath safely warms the ethanol to its boiling point (~78°C) without ignition risk.

Starch Test Results & Conclusion

Look at the leaf on your petri dish: the areas exposed to sunlight turned deep blue-black, proving starch is present. The middle strip covered by black foil stayed yellow-brown, proving no starch was made without light!
